# Decision Log

## 001 — Advanced SOC positioning
The portfolio targets Advanced SOC Analyst roles rather than frontend or full-stack roles.

## 002 — Project-first homepage
IOC Workbench is the primary proof of capability and receives a dedicated case-study route.

## 003 — Viewable resume
The resume is available as an HTML page. PDF download is secondary.

## 004 — Clear section names
Use familiar labels such as Projects, Experience, About, and Contact. Avoid themed labels such as “Case Files.”

## 005 — Design direction
Dark, calm, technical, and product-oriented. Avoid hacker clichés and decorative motion.
